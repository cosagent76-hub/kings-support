Using Kings

App Guide

Kings is a scorekeeper for a real card game. The app tracks players, wild ranks, scores, history, and support details while the cards stay on the table.

Download on the App Store Learn the Rules Get Support

Common App Actions

Start a gameOpen Kings, start a new game, choose 2 to 6 players, and confirm the dealer order.
Learn the rulesUse Learn To Play during setup or from Settings when someone needs a refresher.
Check what is wildLook at the current hand in the scoring flow or scoreboard before entering scores.
Score a handWhen a hand ends, tap Score Hand and enter each player's points. The player who went out scores 0.
Review History and RecordsUse History to review completed games. Use Records to see player stats and table bragging rights.
Copy Support DetailsGo to Settings > About Kings > Copy Support Details before opening a support request.
