Privacy Policy

Effective July 31, 2026. This page explains how Kings handles data in the iOS app.

Summary

  • Tracking: No.
  • Kings does not use ads.
  • Kings does not use third-party analytics tools.
  • Kings does not use third-party crash tools.
  • Kings does not track users across apps or websites.
  • Kings does not sell user data.
  • SCIM Ventures does not run a separate backend server for Kings.
  • Player profiles use names, colors, initials, and optional built-in icons.
  • Optional Family Scorebook sharing uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit systems.

Data Stored by Kings

Kings stores the score data you enter in the app. This may include player names, player profile settings, game results, hand scores, game history, and player stats.

Kings uses this data to keep score, show history, show stats, and support optional Family Scorebook sharing when you turn that on.

iCloud and CloudKit

Kings may use Apple's iCloud and CloudKit for Family Scorebook sharing. This is optional.

If you turn on iCloud sharing, finalized game history, roster/player profile information, scorebook metadata, and summary stats may be stored in or shared through Apple's iCloud service. That sharing uses your Apple account.

In-progress games stay on the device while you are playing. Completed games sync after they are saved and finalized.

SCIM Ventures does not run a separate account system or backend server for Kings.

Data and iCloud FAQ

What stays on my device?

In-progress games stay on your device while you are playing. If iCloud is unavailable, Kings keeps working locally.

What can use iCloud?

If you turn on sharing, completed scorebook history, player profile information, roster data, scorebook metadata, and summary stats may use Apple's iCloud and CloudKit service.

Do I need a Kings account?

No. Kings does not create a separate Kings account. Optional Family Scorebook sharing uses Apple's iCloud sharing flow and your Apple account.

Does SCIM Ventures sell or share my data?

No. SCIM Ventures does not sell user data or share it for ads.

Does Kings use tracking?

No. Kings does not track you across apps or websites.

Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

Kings does not include ad tools, third-party analytics tools, or third-party tracking tools.

Kings does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Support Requests

If you ask for help through the public GitHub support form, what you type may be public on GitHub.

The support form is optional. It is not part of the Kings app, and it is not used for app data collection.

Do not include private personal details, private family details, full scorebook screenshots, or anything else you do not want public.

Children's Privacy

Kings is a scorekeeper for a family card game. It is not made to collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may change when Kings changes. The date at the top of this page will show the latest update.

Contact

For help, visit the Kings Support page. GitHub support requests are public, so do not include private details.
