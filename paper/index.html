Kings App or Paper Score Sheet?

Paper works. Kings helps when wild cards, running totals, saved history, and score questions start interrupting the table.

Use Paper When

  • You want every phone away from the table.
  • Everyone already knows the hand order, wild rank, and scoring flow.
  • You do not care about saved game history or player stats after the game.

Use Kings When

  • The table keeps asking what is wild, what each person scored, or who is winning.
  • You want the app to track hand order, current wild rank, running totals, and final standings.
  • You want completed games saved to history for later bragging rights, records, or family scorebook review.
  • More than one person may need to enter scores during the game.

What Kings Replaces

Score sheet mathKings adds hand scores into running totals.
Wild card remindersKings shows the current wild rank as the game moves from Aces through Kings.
Final standingsKings saves completed games and shows the winner after the last hand.
Lost score sheetsKings keeps completed history in the app.

What Kings Does Not Replace

Kings does not replace the physical cards, the table, house rules, or the conversation. It is a scorekeeper for the real game.
