What's New in Kings 1.3.0

The current App Store release adds Learn To Play, improves History and Records, refreshes Settings, and polishes iPad layouts.

Download on the App Store How to Play View Screenshots

Learn To Play

Kings now includes a native Learn To Play guide for learning the table rules during setup or anytime from Settings.

History, Records, and Settings

The release improves History and Records, refreshes Settings with clearer scorebook, sharing, help, display, and launch controls, and keeps saved game review easier to understand after game night.

iPad and Scoring Polish

iPad layouts are improved across onboarding, scoring, History, and Settings. iPhone gameplay stays portrait with tighter hand-entry polish.

App Store Release Notes

This update adds Learn To Play, a native in-app guide for learning Kings during setup or anytime from Settings. It also improves History and Records, refreshes Settings with clearer scorebook, sharing, help, display, and launch controls, improves iPad layouts across onboarding, scoring, History, and Settings, and keeps iPhone gameplay in portrait with tighter hand-entry polish.

Latest version: 1.3.0. Latest release: August 14, 2026.

Still Built for Real Cards

Kings is a scorekeeper for the physical card game. It does not replace the cards or turn Kings into an online playable game.
