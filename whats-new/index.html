What's New in Kings 1.2.1

Kings 1.2.1 adds Family Scorebook sharing through iCloud, plus guided first-run help and setup/scoring polish.

Download on the App Store Family Scorebook Help

Family Scorebook Sharing

Family Scorebook sharing lets you invite family or friends to join a shared scorebook through Apple's iCloud sharing flow. Each person can claim the player profile that represents them on their own device, and completed game history can stay connected across the shared scorebook.

This is optional. You can still keep score locally on one device without creating a separate Kings account.

Guided First-Run Help

Kings now has more helpful first-run guidance for new players and returning families setting up the app on a device.

Setup and Scoring Polish

This release also includes polish around setup and scoring so the app does a better job staying out of the way while the table plays.

Still Built for Real Cards

Kings is a scorekeeper for the physical card game. It does not replace the cards or turn Kings into an online playable game.
