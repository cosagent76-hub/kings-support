Kings Table FAQ

Use this when someone asks a rules question mid-game. These answers match the standard Kings help flow on this site.

Setup and Turns

How many people can play?Kings works best with 2 to 6 players.
How many cards does everyone get?Deal 7 cards to each player. The dealer gets 8 and takes the first turn.
Does the dealer draw first?No. The dealer already has 8 cards, so the dealer plays if possible and discards 1 card to start the discard pile.
What do I do on my turn?Draw 1 card, play cards if you can, then discard 1 card unless your play used your last card.

Sets, Runs, and Opening

What is a set?A set is 3 or 4 cards with the same rank. Suits do not matter. With one deck, a set cannot have more than 4 cards total.
What is a run?A run is 3 or more cards in order, all in the same suit. Aces are low, so A-2-3 works. Q-K-A does not.
Can I add to someone else's cards right away?No. First lay down your own set or run. This is called opening. You may open and add to another meld on the same turn.
Can I add more than one card?Yes, as long as every card you add creates a legal set or run and you have already opened.

Wild Cards

What is wild?Jokers are always wild. The wild rank changes each hand: hand 1 Aces, hand 2 Twos, and so on through hand 13 Kings.
How do wilds look in a set?Real cards stand upright. The wild card is placed sideways across the bottom so everyone can see it is acting as another rank.
How do wilds look in a run?The wild stays upright in the spot where the missing card belongs.
Can wilds touch in a run?No. Substitute wilds cannot sit next to each other in a run.
Can I replace a wild?Yes, if you have the real card a wild represents in a run. You may take the wild into your hand, or cover it and leave the wild underneath.

Going Out and Scoring

Do I need a final discard to go out?No. If your play uses your last card, the hand ends right away.
What if I can go out?If you can go out, you must go out. The player who goes out scores 0 for that hand.
How do cards score?Aces count 1, cards 2-10 count their number, and Jacks, Queens, and Kings count 10. Jokers and the current wild rank count 20 if left in your hand.
What if the draw pile runs out?Use your table rule. A common approach is to shuffle the discard pile into a new draw pile while keeping the top discard visible.
